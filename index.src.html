The thesis

Most advertising spends its life buying attention nobody offered. Games spent twenty years teaching people to ask for it. A player who has just missed the ramp does not experience your thirty seconds as an interruption — he experiences it as the price of another go. A price he named, and paid, on purpose. Everything else on this page is plumbing.

Psycho-logic

You can't make an interruption welcome. You can put it where somebody was already saying yes.

The thirty seconds,
in order

Five things happen between a wrecked car and a paid impression. Only one of them is an ad.

01 — Crash

Failure is the product

The run ends in a wreck. That is not the sad part of the session, it is the reason he installed it. Arousal is at its highest and he is leaning in, not leaving.

02 — Ask

The only unit with a yes in it

He taps a button to see your ad. Consent isn't inferred from a scroll depth — it's a deliberate tap, logged per impression.

03 — Watch

Whole phone, sound up

Not a 300×250 in a gutter next to a recipe. The entire device, held 30cm from a face, audio already on because the game needs it.

04 — Reward

Your brand buys the next go

The ad is the reason the game continues. Whatever he got — the fuel, the truck, the retry — you gave it to him. Nobody resents the sponsor of a favour.

05 — Auction

One hop, and it's ours

OpenRTB 2.6 straight out of our own SDK. DIRECT in sellers.json. No chain of intermediaries quietly taking a cut of your CPM.

What you can audit

  • Every bid request carries our own bundle ID and app-ads.txt entry.
  • Viewability is a property of the unit, not a vendor's estimate — the ad is the screen.
  • Measurement to the IAB Tech Lab / MRC in-game guidelines; MMP postbacks to AppsFlyer or Adjust.
  • SKAdNetwork and AdAttributionKit on iOS. No IDFA required for us to sell it.

What you won't find

  • Cookies. There were never any to lose.
  • Arbitraged inventory, made-for-advertising placements, or a second SSP wearing our name.
  • Under-13 traffic in personalised demand — the titles are rated for everyone, so that cohort is age-gated out at the SDK.
  • An auto-playing ad the player never agreed to see.
